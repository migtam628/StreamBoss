# Changelog

Every feature or fix gets its own version, newest first. The heading is the git tag without the `v`
(`## 0.2.3` is tag `v0.2.3`) and its section becomes that release's notes. Betas look like `0.3.0b2`
and sort before their final release. See "Versioning" in README.md.

## 0.3.0b19 - 2026-10-10
- **Anime page hides itself when there is no anime.** Settings > Appearance > Anime page: Auto (in the menus only when the library has anime, the default), Always, or Hidden. It affects the top bar, the Marquee rail, the More menu, the Glass dock, Hub and Index. The page is still there for /anime in Console.
- **Channel tools.** Press and hold a live channel (in any list, the strip in Lounge, search results and Console) for its options: add to My list or a collection, Rename, Pin to the top, move among the pinned channels, Hide, and Undo. Pinned channels come first in every channel list; renamed ones show your name everywhere (the TV guide still matches by the provider's name); hidden ones are in no list. It is kept per profile, never touches the provider, and is in backups. Settings > Source & library > Edited channels lists everything changed, with Undo for each and Undo all, and is where a hidden channel comes back. Movies and series still toggle My list on a long press.
- **My lists.** My list and your collections are in one screen (Hub and Bento open it, and Settings > Source & library > Collections still lists them): chips pick the list, and pressing and holding a title offers Move to the top, Move earlier, Move later and Remove. My list now keeps the order you give it (it used to follow the library's order), and A to Z sorts the view without changing your order. A new list can be made from the screen, and renamed or deleted from its menu. Collections are now in backups, which they were not before.
- **Skip memory and the credits.** If you skip the opening of an episode (Skip ahead, or Skip intro) in its first eight minutes, the same skip is offered on the next episodes of that series, even when the files have no chapters. When a file's chapters mark the credits, the next episode is offered as they start, and the Skip credits button reads Next episode when there is one. Settings > Playback > Next episode at the credits turns that off.
- **AirPlay button (iPhone and iPad).** The player has the system AirPlay button. The player's sound goes to the speaker or TV picked, and the picture follows when the screen is mirrored. Not tried on a real device: tell me what you hear. (iOS builds are not part of the release builds.)
- **Faster with big libraries.** What the filters need to know about a title (its words, year and rating) is worked out once instead of on every keystroke and every comparison of a sort, the Anime lookup is done once per library, and the Movies, Series and Anime filters keep their answer while the list and the filter stay the same. A synthetic 100,000 title library: "Newest first" went from about 4 seconds to under 1, Madlib's mood search from 0.4 to 0.2 seconds, and a second keystroke in the filter box is near instant. The first filter after a library loads still reads every title once.
- **README and promo.** The README has a screenshot of each of the 21 layouts (and six on a phone), and its feature list is up to date. The promo video shows the newer layouts.

## 0.3.0b18 - 2026-10-10
- **Fixes the Android build.** 0.3.0b17's Android release did not build, so there is no b17 release: the casting plugin compiles against an old Android SDK. The patch script now raises every plugin's SDK. Everything from b17 (Lounge, Cast to TV, the grouped layout picker, the fuller diagnostics) is in this version.
- **Four new layouts, replacing four.** Madlib, Matchday, Easy and Wall are in; Coverflow, Library, Orbit and Mood are gone. Anyone using one of the retired layouts opens on Marquee, and nothing else changes. 21 layouts in all.
  - **Madlib.** Home is a sentence: "Tonight I feel like a [movie] that is [funny], [well rated], from [any year]." Each bracket opens a list of its words with how many titles each leaves; the titles that fit are listed underneath, best rated first. Moods ("funny", "scary", "moving", "mind-bending", "family friendly", "true") are looked for in the category name, the title and the plot, so they are only as good as the provider's naming. Light lavender with violet.
  - **Matchday.** Home is today's sport by kick-off, from the TV guide: the match on now first, then the rest in time order, each with the channels that carry it (favorites first, then picture quality), and tabs per sport. On a TV the panel beside the list shows every channel for the highlighted event; OK plays the best one. On a phone a tap lists the channels. A programme on a sports channel counts; on any other channel it needs an "A v B" title that names a sport. Without a guide it lists the sports channels. Near-black with lime.
  - **Easy.** Home is three huge buttons (Watch TV opens your last channel, Movies and shows, Find), a Keep watching bar and a small Settings button. Black, white and yellow, text at least 40 px on a TV, a thick double focus ring. No menu: Back from any screen returns to it.
  - **Wall.** Home is the whole library as one poster wall (Movies, Series or Live), with a details panel for the poster the cursor is on (under the wall on a phone, where the first tap selects and the second opens), the same filter bar as the Movies page, and a zoom button (or hold OK) for close up, normal and zoomed out. Warm brown with amber.
- **Filter Movies and Series.** The Movies and Series pages (in every layout that has them) now have a filter box and a Filters button, like the Live page does. Words in the title or category, a lowest rating (6+, 7+, 8+), a year range (2020s, 2010s, 2000s, older, read from the year in the title where there is one), favorites only, not watched yet, and an order (provider, A to Z, best rated, newest). Movies, Series and Anime each keep their own filter while the app runs.
- **Anime page.** A new screen next to Series (top bar, rail, More menu and Hub and Index; /anime in Console; Settings > Appearance > Open on can start there). It gathers the categories named anime, manga, donghua, shonen, shoujo, seinen, isekai, OVA and similar, and titles tagged "(Anime)", across series, movies and channels, with chips for what it found, the provider's anime categories and the same filter bar. With none it says how the page finds things. Name based, so tell me which category names yours uses if some are missed.

## 0.3.0b17 - 2026-10-10
- **Lounge layout (the twenty-first).** Settings > Appearance > Layout > Lounge. The top half of Home is the channel you were on, playing live and muted, with its number, name and what is on now. Under it, a strip of channels (with the shared filter box and category chips above it): on a TV, moving along the strip changes the picture a moment later; by touch the first tap on a channel puts it on top and a second tap opens it. OK or tapping the picture plays full screen. One preview player is reused for every channel you flick past. Graphite with teal. Settings > Appearance > Live pictures turns the picture off (the top half then shows a play button).
- **Cast to TV (experimental).** The player has a new Cast to TV button. It looks for Chromecasts and TVs with Chromecast built in on the same network, plays the stream on the one you pick (the picture here pauses), and offers pause, resume and stop. It talks to the device directly with no Google SDK, so it is not on the web build, and the TV opens the stream itself: a provider that allows one connection will count two, and streams a Chromecast cannot decode will fail with a message. I could not try it on real hardware, so tell me what happens. For AirPlay use the system's Screen Mirroring; there is no AirPlay picker in the app yet. iOS builds: run `dart run tool/patch_ios.dart` after `flutter create` (it adds the Bonjour and local-network entries Cast needs).
- **Layout picker, tidied.** The layouts are now grouped (Everyday, Live TV, Decide for me, Simple and family, Something different), and after you switch, the top of the picker offers "Not for you? Go back to ...". Choosing a layout still applies it at once, which is the preview.
- **A fuller diagnostics report.** Settings > About > Copy diagnostics now includes the layout and video settings, whether Cast is available, how many countries were found in your live categories, the category names that matched no country, and the kids categories that matched, so I can fix the parsers against real provider names. It still has no addresses or passwords.
- Round-eight concept sheet (Madlib, Matchday, Easy, Wall) is out for you to choose from; nothing was removed from the roster in this version.

## 0.3.0b16 - 2026-10-10
- **Mini player (picture-in-picture inside the app).** In the player, the new Mini player button (or the P key) closes the full screen and keeps the stream going in a small window above every screen, so you can carry on browsing. Drag it to any corner; its buttons are previous and next channel, pause, mute, full screen and close. Tapping the picture (or OK on a remote) opens the full player again with the same stream, without restarting it, and holding OK closes it. A movie keeps its resume position while in the window. On a remote, moving the cursor selects and OK opens full screen; by touch, the first tap on a channel puts it in the preview and a second tap opens it. The Control Room's right-hand pane and Prime Time's header now play the highlighted channel too. Settings > Appearance > Live pictures turns all of these off.
- **Better channel filters.** Live, the Guide and the Control Room all have a Filter channels box (every word has to match the channel or its category, accents ignored) and a Filters button: quality (HD and up, Full HD and up, 4K), country (read from names like "ES | Sports"), favorites only, only channels with TV guide data, and the order (provider, A to Z, best quality first). The filter is shared, so setting it in the Guide is still there in Live. A count shows how many channels are left, and the X clears it.
- **Console layout (the nineteenth).** Settings > Appearance > Layout > Console. Home is a prompt: type and channels, movies and series appear as you type (ranked as the Search screen does), with kind chips and a live picture or poster of the highlighted result beside the list. Start with a slash for a command: /live, /guide, /movies, /series, /search, /settings, /list (My List) and /recent. With nothing typed it lists what you were watching and saved, and your recent searches. Amber on near-black.
- **Deck layout (the twentieth).** Settings > Appearance > Layout > Deck. Home is a deck of twelve picks: first what fits what you have watched and saved ("Because you watched ..."), then well-rated titles, movies and series alternating. Left says "not tonight" (the title stays out for 30 days, per profile), Up saves to My List, OK plays, Down opens the page. On a phone swipe left to skip and right to save, or use the round buttons. At the end of the deck: New deck, or Forget my skips. Cobalt blue with cream cards.
- Not tried on a real Android or TV device yet: the mini player there uses the standard video output, so tell me if the window flickers or stays black on a Fire TV.

## 0.3.0b14 - 2026-10-10
- **Tonight layout (the sixteenth).** Settings > Appearance > Layout > Tonight. Home is an evening planner: one timeline of what is on now, what starts later tonight on the channels you favor or watched lately, the movie or episode you are partway through, and what you saved to My List. ## 0.3.0b13 - 2026-10-09
- **Profile settings.** A profile can have its own settings (Settings > Profiles & PIN > edit a profile > Own settings): layout, colours, text and poster size, start tab, languages, subtitle look, adult and A-Z filters, 24-hour clock, merge duplicates and live pictures. It starts from what is in use, so turning it on changes nothing until you change something. Turning it off goes back to the device settings. Source, network and PIN settings stay shared. Deleting a profile deletes its settings, searches and collections too.
- **Several sources at once.** Settings > Source & library: switch "Also show this source in the library" on for any saved source and its channels, movies and series appear alongside the main one (each plays, shows details and uses catch-up through its own login). Categories with the same name are labelled with the source. The guides are merged. A source that fails to load is skipped, with the reason shown, and the rest still load.
- **Custom themes.** Settings > Appearance > Colors: pick an accent colour from swatches or a hue slider, and a background (Layout's own, Black, Charcoal, Midnight, Forest, Plum or Paper). Layouts that paint their own backdrop (Glass, Mood, Mosaic) keep it and take the accent.
- **Screensaver.** After a few minutes with nothing pressed or touched, and nothing playing, slow columns of posters drift past with the time wandering across the screen so nothing burns in. Any key or touch brings you back (that key does nothing else). Settings > Appearance > Screensaver: Auto (10 minutes on a TV, off elsewhere), Off, or 2 to 30 minutes, with a preview.
- **Subtitle search.** In the player, Subtitles > Search online finds subtitles on OpenSubtitles for the movie or episode (by title and year, or season and episode, in your subtitle language) and loads the one you pick. It needs your own free OpenSubtitles API key, and downloading also needs your OpenSubtitles username and password (Settings > Network & metadata > OpenSubtitles). They stay on the device and are never in backups. Tested against a stand-in server only, not the real service: tell me what it answers.

## 0.3.0b12 - 2026-10-09
- **Better search.** Several words in any order, the start of a word, accents and punctuation ignored, and a small typo forgiven ("nigth" finds "Night"). Results are grouped into Channels, Movies and Series with counts and ranked by how well they match (your My List and recent watches edge ahead). A Filters panel sorts by best match, A to Z or rating, narrows to a rating, and to a category once you pick Channels, Movies or Series. Recent searches come back as chips, per profile.
- **Duplicate channel merging.** When a channel is listed several times (HD, SD, 4K, a backup), the app shows one entry, the best copy that is not known to be offline. If it will not play, or does not start within 15 seconds, the player moves on to the next copy by itself ("Trying another copy of ..."). "US: Fox" and "UK: Fox", and "Sports 1" and "Sports 2", stay separate. Settings > Library & guide > Merge duplicate channels turns it off.
- **Live rewind.** Pause and rewind a live channel as far as the player has kept (about a minute on a TV box, more on a phone or computer): Left and Right on the remote, or the buttons. The top of the controls shows how far behind live you are, and Back to live returns. A channel that keeps nothing says so.
- **Catch-up.** On Xtream channels that keep an archive, Catch-up in the live player lists the last programmes, and the guide offers "Watch from the start" or "Watch from the archive" on a programme. Times are written in the provider's own clock. Not tried against a real provider yet: tell me if a provider's archive does not play.
- **Programme details.** Selecting a programme in the guide that is not on now (or holding OK / long-pressing any) shows its description from the guide, its time and length, and what you can do with it. The guide now loads the last day, for catch-up.
- **Quick channel switcher.** The Channels button (or the C key) in the live player lists every channel in the list you are zapping through, with a filter by name or number.
- **Chapters.** Movies and episodes with chapters get a Chapters list, and a Skip intro or Skip credits button when the chapter playing is named like one (press OK to take it).
- **Collections.** Make your own lists like "Friday movie night" (Add to collection on a movie or series page; Settings > Library & guide > Collections to manage them). They show as shelves on Home. Per profile.
- **Picked for you.** A Home shelf of movies and series you have not seen, chosen from what you watched and saved: the same categories, sequels and titles that share a word, and a lift for good ratings ("Because you watched ...").
- **Real posters.** With a TMDB key, movies and series that came without a poster get one from TMDB (three lookups at a time, remembered). Settings > Network & metadata > Fill in missing posters.
- **Fix:** programme times in the guide and the layouts that show them were written in UTC. They now show in the device's time zone.

## 0.3.0b11 - 2026-10-09
- **Channel check.** Settings > Source & library > Check live channels tests every channel and remembers the ones that do not answer (an error page, a 404, nothing back, a timeout). They show as OFFLINE in the Live list, and "Hide offline channels" removes them everywhere. It reads only the first couple of kilobytes of each stream and hangs up straight away. On a provider login it checks two at a time, because logins limit how many streams may be open; on a public list it checks ten. Apps only: a browser cannot read other sites' streams.
- **Profiles and a PIN lock.** Settings > Profiles & PIN. Each profile keeps its own My List, history and resume positions (the first one, Main, keeps everything you already had). A **Kids** profile shows only categories that look made for children, by name. With more than one profile the app asks "Who's watching?" when it starts. Set a four-digit PIN to lock Settings while a Kids profile is in use, to ask for it when leaving a Kids profile, and to lock any profile you choose. Five wrong tries lock the PIN for 30 seconds. It is a family lock for a shared screen, not high security.
- **Live pictures.** Cable Box shows the channel behind its big number, and every Mosaic tile plays its channel (only the tile with the sound is audible; on a phone only the big tile plays). They play only while you can see them, wait a moment after you change channel, and fall back to the old look if anything fails. Settings > Appearance > Live pictures turns them off.
- **Gzipped guides.** `.xml.gz` XMLTV guides are unpacked and read in the background, so large guides load faster and more providers' guides work.
- Free-list and provider fix from 0.3.0b10 is unchanged.

## 0.3.0b10 - 2026-10-08
- Fix: the free public channels (and any other `https://` playlist or provider) failed on phones, tablets, TVs and desktops with "Invalid request method". The connection helper that tries IPv4 first handed Dart a plain socket and never started TLS, so servers received unencrypted data on port 443 and refused it. HTTPS now does its TLS handshake. Plain `http://` addresses and the web build were not affected.
- A test now runs a real local HTTPS server so this cannot come back.

## 0.2.6 - 2026-10-05
- Smoother video on Fire TV and Android TV, and a fix for movies closing the app. The player used to copy every decoded frame through the GPU, which a TV stick can't keep up with for movies (4K frames need a lot of memory), and it made live TV laggy. On a TV the video now goes straight from the hardware decoder to the screen ("Hardware surface"). Settings > Playback > Video output lets you switch back to Compatible (GPU), which is also what the app falls back to by itself if playback closes while starting. The surface output can't draw embedded subtitles or shaders.
- When a video can't start because the network can't look up the server that hosts it (common: the catalog loads but movies come from a different address that a carrier or filtering DNS blocks), the player now says so and what to try, instead of suggesting a different decoder.
- The player's memory buffer is smaller on TVs (16 MB instead of 32 MB each way).
- If playback closes while starting, the app now steps down one level at a time: first the standard video output, then software decoding (it used to jump straight to software, which made live TV slow after a crash).
