The player. Not the provider.

StreamBoss plays the Xtream or M3U service you already have, on the TV, phone and computer you already own. It carries nothing of its own: the signal path begins at your provider and ends at your screen.

Player only · no content included
Fig. 1 · Signal path

What is in the chain, and what is not

Your login stays on your device. StreamBoss opens the stream the provider hands it and plays it.

It does

  • Play live TV, movies and series from your provider
  • Show the programme guide, catch-up and reminders
  • Remember positions, lists and profiles
  • Sync between your devices, through a server you run

It does not

  • Supply channels, movies or subscriptions
  • Ask you to make a StreamBoss account
  • Send your provider login anywhere
  • Charge for a layout or a feature unlock
